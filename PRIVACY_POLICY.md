# Privacy Policy

**Gift Card Manager** is a fully offline, private application.

- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal, financial, or analytics data. There are no tracking SDKs, ads, or remote servers.
- **Local Storage:** All card details, balances, and history remain exclusively on your device, encrypted in a local database.
- **Biometrics:** If you enable App Lock, fingerprint or screen lock verification is handled entirely by your device's operating system (`BiometricPrompt`). The app never accesses or stores your biometric data.
- **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes all stored cards and encryption keys immediately and permanently.
